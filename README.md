Project Title: Nebusiwe Wellness Centre

Student information: Karabo Siyamukela Nkwanyana

Project overview:

Nebusiwe Wellness Centre is a private clinic located in the heart of Bakerton Springs that provides affordable private healthcare to everyone in the community and surrounding areas.
The business had no consistent online presence. I created am early set of 5 html pages but only used inline styling. Part one focuses on the html structure, buliding sematic, valid, bug free markup across all pages. Part two
focuses on CSS styling with a single shared stylesheet shared across all web pages. it introduces a consistant visual identity and making every page responsive. A sixth page was added which was the enquiries page what had a 
form for enquirires. the page was then layered into every other page to create a seamless transition through pages. The finished site consisits of 6 pages, Home, About Us, Services, Galary, Contact us and enquiries, all sharing 
one global stylesheet, consistant navigation structure and one consistent design.

Goals:

• Build an online presence: give the Centre a professional, trustworthy digital home that reflects 
its patient-centred brand. 
• Generate leads and enquiries: convert visitors into booked appointments and phone/email 
enquiries. 
• Enable online bookings: let patients request or schedule appointments without having to call 
during business hours. 
• Provide reliable health information: give patients easy access to service details, operating 
hours, location and health resources. 
• Increase reach and traffic: attract new patients in the surrounding community through search 
engines and social media links. 
• Build trust and credibility: showcase the founder's story, qualified staff and patient 
testimonials. 

Objectives:
. Deliver valid, sematic HTML across all pages with no broken links
. Apply a single consistent stylesheet
. Add a working enquiry mechanism 

Key features and functionality
• Homepage: clinic overview, hero banner with key messaging and quick links to services. 
• About Us: the Centre's history, founder Busisiwe Kwanyana's background, mission and vision. 
• Services page: a clear list of medical and wellness services offered, with short descriptions of 
each. 
• Contact Us page: physical address with embedded map, phone number, email address, 
operating hours and a general enquiry form. 
• Gallery: where you can see our posters and pictures
• Enquires: a general enquiry form.
